Frequently Asked Questions

Will my videos get approved on microstock sites?
Our exports are calibrated for Adobe Stock and Shutterstock — 95% of videos made with MotionStock Studio are approved.
How do I activate Pro with a license key?
Choose the License Key tab above, paste your key, and press Activate Pro. Your Pro badge and expiry date will appear in the Studio.
Is rendering really free?
Yes. Rendering is free and unlimited at every resolution — 720p, 1080p and 4K. Pro is a status badge with an expiry date.
What are tokens?
You get 1000 tokens when you sign up. Downloading a video costs 10 tokens (4K costs 20). Refer a friend — when they sign up with your link, you both get 1000 tokens. Rendering and preview are always free and unlimited.
Does the preview match the rendered video?
Yes. The preview uses the exact same layout as the render, so what you see is what you get — including speed and zoom settings.
